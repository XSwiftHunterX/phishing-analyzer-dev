About

A community-driven platform for identifying, analyzing, and learning from suspicious messages.

What This Platform Is

This platform is designed to help users identify, understand, and report suspicious messages such as phishing emails, scam texts, and other deceptive communications.

Users can submit suspicious messages, receive AI-assisted analysis, and explore reports shared by the community in order to better recognize common scam tactics and warning signs.

Why It Exists

Phishing and online scams continue to evolve, and many harmful messages now look more convincing than ever. This site was created to make phishing awareness more practical by combining user-submitted examples with automated analysis and moderation.

The goal is to give people a place where they can learn from real examples, compare suspicious messages, and become more confident at spotting red flags before harm is done.

What Users Can Do

  • Submit suspicious messages for review
  • Receive AI-assisted analysis of possible phishing red flags
  • Upload screenshots to provide additional context
  • Browse and search community-submitted reports
  • Compare messages with similar reports
  • Participate through comments, likes, and reporting tools

Moderation and Safety

Content submitted to the platform may go through moderation and safety review before becoming visible to others. This helps reduce abuse, spam, unsafe uploads, and misleading content.

Depending on the submission and review outcome, some content may be approved, held for review, rejected, or removed in order to protect the platform and its users.

Important Disclaimer

AI-generated analysis on this platform is provided for informational and educational purposes only. Automated results may be incomplete or inaccurate and should not be treated as professional security, legal, or financial advice.

Users should always use their own judgment and verify suspicious messages through official sources whenever possible.

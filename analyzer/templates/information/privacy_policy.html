Privacy Policy

This page explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how content is handled on this platform.

Information We Collect

When you use this platform, we may collect information that you provide directly, including:

  • Account information (such as username and email address)
  • Profile details (such as bio or profile image, if provided)
  • Submitted message content and sender details
  • Uploaded screenshots or images
  • Comments, likes, and reports

We may also collect limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the platform.

How Information Is Used

  • To provide and operate the platform
  • To analyze submitted messages using AI-assisted tools
  • To review and moderate content for safety and policy compliance
  • To improve features, usability, and security
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam, or misuse

Automated Processing and AI Analysis

Submitted content may be processed using automated systems, including AI models, to help identify potential phishing indicators and generate analysis results.

These systems may also be used for moderation, safety checks, image review, and relevance validation. Automated results are not guaranteed to be accurate.

Public Content

Content submitted to the platform may become publicly visible if it is approved for display. This may include message content, sender details, and associated screenshots.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal, private, or sensitive information. You are responsible for the content you choose to upload.

Screenshots and Sensitive Information

Uploaded images may be reviewed for safety, relevance, and potential sensitive information. In some cases, automated tools may attempt to detect or handle personal data within images.

However, no system is perfect. Users should take care not to upload screenshots that contain sensitive personal information unless necessary.

Data Retention

Information may be retained as long as necessary to operate the platform, support moderation, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations. Content may remain available unless it is removed or deleted.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user data and maintain platform security. However, no system can guarantee complete security, and users should use the platform with this understanding.

User Responsibility

By using this platform, you acknowledge that you are responsible for the content you submit and for ensuring that you have the right to share it.

You should avoid uploading private, confidential, or sensitive information unless it is necessary and appropriate.

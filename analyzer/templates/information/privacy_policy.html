Privacy Policy

How your information is collected, used, and handled on this platform.

Automated results are not guaranteed to be accurate and should be used for informational purposes only.

No system is perfect. Avoid uploading sensitive personal data unless necessary.

Information may be retained as needed to operate the platform, support moderation, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user data, but no system can guarantee complete security.

User Responsibility

You are responsible for the content you submit and must ensure you have the right to share it.

Avoid uploading private, confidential, or sensitive information unless appropriate.

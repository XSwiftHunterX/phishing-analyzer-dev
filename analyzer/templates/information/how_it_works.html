How It Works

Learn how messages are submitted, analyzed, reviewed, and shared on the platform.

1. Submit a Suspicious Message

Users can submit messages they believe may be phishing or scams. This can include emails, text messages, or other forms of communication.

  • Enter the message content
  • Provide sender details (if available)
  • Select a classification (phishing, legitimate, or unsure)
  • Optionally upload a screenshot for additional context

2. AI-Assisted Analysis

After submission, the platform uses AI to analyze the message and identify potential phishing indicators such as suspicious links, urgency tactics, or unusual requests.

The analysis may include a summary, highlighted red flags, and a confidence-based assessment. This information is intended to help users better understand why a message may be risky.

3. Moderation and Review

Submissions may go through automated and/or manual review to ensure they meet platform guidelines and are safe to display.

  • Content may be approved, rejected, or held for review
  • Images may be checked for relevance and safety
  • Potentially sensitive information may be flagged or handled

4. View and Learn from Reports

Approved submissions become part of the platform’s searchable database, where users can explore real examples of suspicious messages.

  • Browse recent or popular reports
  • Search by message content, sender, or user
  • Compare messages with similar reports
  • Learn common phishing tactics and patterns

5. Community Interaction

Users can interact with content to help improve the platform and contribute to shared awareness.

  • Comment on reports
  • Like helpful submissions
  • Report content that may violate guidelines

Important Note

AI-generated analysis is provided for informational purposes only and may not always be accurate. Users should independently verify suspicious messages through trusted and official sources before taking action.

The platform is designed to support awareness and learning, not to replace professional judgment or security tools.

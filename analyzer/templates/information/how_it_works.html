How It Works

Learn how messages are submitted, analyzed, reviewed, and shared on the platform.

From submission to analysis and community learning, each step is designed to help identify and understand suspicious messages more effectively.

1. Submit a Suspicious Message

Users can submit messages they believe may be phishing or scams, including emails, text messages, or other communications.

Enter the message content
Provide sender details (if available)
Select a classification
Upload a screenshot (optional)

2. AI-Assisted Analysis

After submission, AI analyzes the message to identify phishing indicators such as suspicious links, urgency tactics, or unusual requests.

  • Summary of the message
  • Highlighted red flags
  • Confidence-based assessment

3. Moderation and Review

Submissions may go through automated and/or manual review to ensure they meet platform guidelines and are safe to display.

  • Content may be approved, rejected, or held for review
  • Images are checked for safety and relevance
  • Sensitive information may be flagged or handled

4. View and Learn from Reports

Approved submissions become part of a searchable database of real-world examples.

  • Browse recent or popular reports
  • Search by message content, sender, or user
  • Compare messages with similar reports
  • Learn common phishing patterns

5. Community Interaction

Users contribute to shared awareness by interacting with reports.

  • Comment on reports
  • Like helpful submissions
  • Report problematic content

Important Note

AI-generated analysis is provided for informational purposes only and may not always be accurate.

Always verify suspicious messages through trusted and official sources before taking action.

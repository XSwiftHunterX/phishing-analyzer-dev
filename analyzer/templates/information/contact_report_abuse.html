Contact & Report Abuse

Get help, report issues, or raise concerns about content or behavior on the platform.

Whether you need support, want to report harmful content, or have privacy concerns, this page will guide you to the right action.

What do you need help with?

Report Content

Use built-in report buttons on messages or comments whenever possible for faster moderation review.

Contact Support

Reach out for account issues, technical problems, or general questions about the platform.

Report Abuse or Harmful Content

Please report content or behavior involving harassment, impersonation, privacy violations, or misuse of the platform.

  • Harassment or abusive behavior
  • Impersonation or misleading identity
  • Spam or platform misuse
  • Suspicious or harmful activity

Include as much detail as possible (usernames, links, screenshots) to help speed up review.

How to Report Content

1. Use the built-in report button on messages or comments
2. For broader issues, contact support directly
3. Provide links, usernames, or details when possible

Privacy & Takedown Requests

If content includes personal information, violates your rights, or should be removed, please contact us with the relevant details.

Requests involving privacy, impersonation, or misuse are reviewed carefully and prioritized when appropriate.
